# Two-cut rank, partial-transpose rank, and sum-of-squares complexity of biquadratic forms

Unconditional, kernel-checked Lean 4 results on the matrix two-cut rank `TC_K(M) = min { rank A + rank W : M = A + W^Γ }`, Rossman–Zhu's partial-transpose rank `PT-rank`, and the sum-of-squares complexity `SoS_K(Q_M)` of biquadratic forms:

- **Exact dictionary and Rossman–Zhu §3.2:** over every field `K`, `PT-rank_K(M) = TC_K(M)` and `Prod_K(Q_M) ≤ TC_K(M)`; when `char K ≠ 2` and `M` is fully symmetric, `TC_K(M) ≤ 2 SoS_K(Q_M)` and `TC_K(M) ≤ 4 Prod_K(Q_M)`; when `char K ≠ 2` and `√(-1) ∈ K` (for arbitrary `M`), `SoS_K(Q_M) ≤ 2 Prod_K(Q_M) ≤ 2 TC_K(M) = 2 PT-rank_K(M)`. Three explicit matrices (`M₁, M₃ ∈ M_4(K)` and `M₂ ∈ M_16(K)`) refute the printed statements of Rossman–Zhu (arXiv:2512.01227v1) Lemma 3.13 (`SoS(Q_M) ≤ PT-rank(M)`) and both inequalities of Proposition 3.16 (`SoS(Q_M) - 1 ≤ PT-rank(M) ≤ 2^(d-1) SoS(Q_M)`) at `d = 2` over every field of characteristic ≠ 2, proving that the factor `2` in `SoS_K(Q_M) ≤ 2 PT-rank_K(M)` is tight in general. Whether `SoS_ℂ(Q_M) ≤ PT-rank_ℂ(M)` holds for *fully symmetric* `M` remains open.
- **Characteristic-free crossing bounds:** over *every* field `K` (including characteristic `2`), `TC_K(r, s) ≥ max(r, s)` for all `r, s ≥ 1`, `TC_K(r, s) ≥ max(r, s) + 1` for all `r, s ≥ 3`, `TC_K(2, s) = 2⌈s/2⌉`, `TC_K(r, s) ≤ 1 + rs - m(m+1)/2` (`m = min(r, s)`), and `TC_K(3, 3) = 4`.
- **Explicit exact and border witnesses:** self-dual Clifford bound `TC_K(d, n) ≤ 2d` (`char K ≠ 2`), border-to-exact conversion `TC_K(r, s) ≤ (k + 1)(a + b)` over every field, explicit `±1` exact schemes `TC_K(4, 5) ≤ 8`, `TC_K(4, 6) ≤ 8`, `TC_K(8, 8) ≤ 16` over every field, and an explicit pole-order-2 border decomposition `bTC_{K,2}(4, 4) ≤ 6` over every field.
- **Reflection-type word-span tensor powers:** reduction to the `p`-biased reflection operator `T_{-1}` in `L²(μ_p)`; a Valuation Theorem proving that when `λ = a/b` has a prime `ℓ ∣ (a - b)` with `ℓ ∤ a`, the layer grouping attains the exact minimum `∑_T min(μ_p(T), μ_{1-p}(T))` over all `ℤ_(ℓ)`-coefficient word spans at every tensor power `K`; tensorising Farkas-certificate packings including a kernel-checked 22-certificate `K = 4, p = 1/4` rational packing of exact value `57/256`; and a no-go theorem proving that for every shape `r, s ≥ 2` and every reflection-type decomposition over `ℝ`, no word-span tensor power beats the rate `√(1 + q²)` (`q = log min(r,s) / log max(r,s)`).

What is *not* machine-checked: the word-span no-go theorem applies to word-span decompositions of reflection-type diagonalisable bases over `ℝ`, not to general non-word-span decompositions, non-reflection spectra, non-diagonalisable bases, or border families.
